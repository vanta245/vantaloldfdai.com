Who we are

Vanta takes its name from the deepest black: a color that absorbs everything and lets what matters stand out. That idea shapes how we think, design, and build.

We believe great products come from focus. Fewer features, sharper details, and an obsession with how things feel in your hands and look in the world.

We’re a small team with a clear direction, and we’re getting ready to share what we’ve been working on.
